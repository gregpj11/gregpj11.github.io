About Me

Greg Johnson has been a professional sports journalist for more than a decade with The Trentonian and affiliated local newspapers within MediaNews Group. He primary covers the New York Giants, the Somerset Patriots (New York Yankees’ Double-A affiliate), high school and college athletics. Over his career, his beat coverage has taken him on-the-ground to major events including Super Bowl LIV in Miami and the 2026 MLB All-Star Week in Philadelphia.
